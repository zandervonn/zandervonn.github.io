How to play

One rule. Endless collisions.

Match identical planets to create the next world and build a bigger score.

  1. 01 Aim

    Touch and drag to line up your shot around the gravity well.

  2. 02 Launch

    Release and let motion, gravity, and a little nerve do the rest.

  3. 03 Merge

    Collide matching planets, chain combos, and climb toward the Sun.
